Scope: 12 smallcase publishers chosen from smallcase.com's public manager pages. Selection: the largest publishers by basket count plus the names you listed, to the extent they exist on smallcase. Every record is pulled from smallcase.com/manager/<slug> (the `managerData` JSON on each page: legal name, SEBI number, CIN, grievance officer, disclosure text, basket count, onboarding date). ## Read this first: what "verified" means here

**I could not query SEBI's own registry or order search.** SEBI's intermediary page (`sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/other/OtherAction.do?doRecognisedFpi=yes&intmId=14`, RA list) loads, but only its first 25 alphabetical rows. Name and number search runs through a POST call (`/sebiweb/ajax/other/getintmfpiinfo.jsp`) that SEBI's firewall rejected as "Unauthorized Activity" when I sent it from a script. I did not try to get around that block. SEBI's order listing pages likewise load only their default page. This means:

- No registration below is checked against SEBI's registry. "Cross-checked" means the same number appears on smallcase's manager record and on the firm's own disclosure page. Both are self-reported by the manager.
- "No adverse found" means no hit in repeated web searches of sebi.gov.in orders and news. That is weak evidence of absence. The one SEBI order I did find, I read in full (Marcellus below).
- SCORES complaint pages could not be searched by entity. The only complaint figures I saw are the firms' own published tables (Wright only).
- Nothing was filled from memory. Founder credentials are "claimed" unless stated otherwise.

**Before paying anyone, do this yourself in a browser (2 minutes per firm):** go to SEBI > Intermediaries > Recognised Intermediaries > Research Analysts (or Investment Advisers / Portfolio Managers), search the registration number, and check name, status and validity date. Then search SEBI > Enforcement > Orders for the entity and principal.

## Notes on the red flag and the discrepancies

1. **Marcellus.** The order is real and I read it in full. Its main fact is that the Ambit-era conduct concerned a research report, which is the very activity a manager does for you now. Mitigating: the firm discloses it itself, it was settled in 2020, and Mukherjea's penalty was a settlement charge (the order is "without prejudice" to SEBI reopening if any representation proves untrue). The 2023 inspection letter was about the majority partner and the firm says it is closed. This is your call: weigh it against the firm's disclosure, which is more candid than any other I read. Only 2 baskets on smallcase.
2. **Windmill** is the platform's own affiliate. No individual analyst is named. I found no external evidence of ownership beyond the firm's disclosure text on smallcase.
3. **Wright** shows multiple internal inconsistencies on its own pages (addresses, membership body, "Mumbai-based" vs Lucknow, "10+" vs "17+" years). They are probably sloppy copy, not evidence of anything, but you should ask. The Investowryght order (Sept 2026) is a different entity with a similar name. I found no connection and none was stated in the Moneylife article.
4. **Growth Investing** entity incorporated 2025 vs smallcase presence since 2023.
5. Complaint (SCORES) data: only Wright published it on a page I could read. Ask every shortlisted firm for its latest monthly and annual complaint tables, which RAs and IAs must publish.
