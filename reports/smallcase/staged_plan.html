When to look again

  • Month 3 and month 6: compare each sleeve with a plain Nifty 500 index fund bought on the same dates. A sleeve that trails it after costs for 12 months needs a reason to stay.
  • Manager change: if Sonam Udasi leaves Tata India Consumer or Sailesh Raj Bhan leaves Nippon India Multi Cap, review that sleeve the same week.
  • Energy Tracker: read each model-portfolio PDF after a rebalance. If the holdings or the share of power utilities change a lot, review before approving anything.
  • Autos: the sector is down about 15% since August and margins have not turned. Weak months are expected; the pause rule decides what you do.

What is and is not known

  • Fund records come from AMFI NAV history and AMC factsheets, to 8 Oct 2026. Most strong numbers come from the 2021 and 2023 rallies; several funds are flat since December 2024.
  • Manager credentials are claims from the firms' own pages. SEBI's registry could not be searched by script, so check Windmill Capital's registration (INH200007645) yourself on SEBI's Research Analysts list.
  • The Energy Tracker is a hand-picked basket of 11 stocks, about 57% oil, gas and coal and 43% power utilities, mostly government-owned. It has no named analyst.
  • Nothing here predicts returns. This approach seeks sector exposure at low cost, not multi-baggers.
