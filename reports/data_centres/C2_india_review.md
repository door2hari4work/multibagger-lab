Both press and yfinance are arithmetically right; they use different bases. Q1 FY26 included Rs 25.2 cr after-tax late-payment surcharge from a discontinued energy business (about Rs 2.16 a share, stated by management). The like-for-like fall is **-15.9%**.

Depreciation tripled (Rs 6.3 cr vs 2.1 cr) as the Chennai and edge data centres entered operation.
**Implication:** treasury income was about a quarter of PBT (Q1 FY27 Rs 29.1 cr of Rs 118.5 cr; FY26 Rs 149.5 cr of Rs 575.1 cr), and it is shrinking as cash is spent. Headline P/E flatters the operating business (the original report said this but did not quantify it).

## 3. AVOID names and the reverse-DCF logic

Method check (reproduced): price x (1+r)^5 = TTM EPS x (1+g)^5 x exit P/E. The algebra is correct. Weaknesses: TTM base understates names whose consensus EPS jumps (Hitachi FY27E is +48% on FY26), a fixed 20x exit is low for structurally high-multiple capital goods, 13% is an arbitrary hurdle, and dilution is ignored. So I re-ran it under generous inputs.

Required 5-year EPS CAGR from TTM EPS (2026-10-19 prices; yfinance TTM and consensus):

| Name | P/E TTM / FY27E / FY28E | Exit 20x, 13% | Exit 30x, 12% | Exit 40x, 10% | Consensus EPS growth FY26 to FY28E (a year) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hitachi Energy India | 111 / 84 / 57 | 59% | 46% | 35% | about 48% |
| ABB India | 95 / 89 / 67 | 54% | 41% | 31% | about 15% (trailing distorted by a Mar-26 exceptional gain) |
| Siemens Energy India | 68 / 61 / 51 | 44% | 32% | 22% | about 35% |
| CG Power | 108 / 84 / 61 | 58% | 45% | 34% | about 33% |
| GE Vernova T&D | 77 / 64 / 49 | 48% | 35% | 25% | about 28% |

Findings:
- **Hitachi, ABB, CG Power stay AVOID.** Even at a 40x exit and a 10% hurdle they need 31-35% EPS growth for five years; ABB's own consensus growth is about 15%.
- **Siemens Energy India and GE Vernova T&D are borderline, not wrongly dismissed.** Under the generous case (22% and 25%) the required growth is inside what consensus shows for the next two years, but both carry structural issues the multiple does not price: 75% and 51% parent control (thin float), GE Vernova T&D debtor days 194. Re-test after their next prints; no trigger now.
- Quality is not the dismissal reason. Hitachi's Q1 FY27 is real (revenue +71%, PAT +124%, backlog Rs 32,222 cr per the original, promoter 71.31%, no pledge). It is a price call.
- **The original Hitachi re-rate trigger is too loose.** Rs 22,800 equals 45x FY28E; at a 30x exit and 12% hurdle it still needs about 32% a year for 3.5 years beyond FY28 (INFERENCE). A level that implies roughly 23% (35x FY28E, about Rs 17,700, -38%) is a more honest "cheap enough"; otherwise leave it as AVOID.
- Because these stocks are priced for perfection, a hyperscaler capex cut or a rate shock would hit them harder than Techno; that argues against swapping Techno for them.
