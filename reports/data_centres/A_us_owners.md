## 1. Bottom line
1. **Real businesses at fair-but-full prices: Equinix (EQIX), Digital Realty (DLR), Iron Mountain (IRM).** None is cheap. They trade at 19-23x 2026 AFFO/core FFO, i.e. an AFFO yield of 4.4-5.2% against a 5.10% risk-free 10-year. You are paying for 10-14% per-share AFFO growth. Only EQIX looks to have a workable margin for error (see 4B).
2. **American Tower (AMT) is the cheapest multiple (16.7x AFFO, 3.9% yield) but is not a data-centre bet**: CoreSite is ~11% of revenue and its segment operating profit halved y/y. Treat it as a tower/rates stock.
3. **Financing-dependent bets: CoreWeave, Applied Digital, IREN, Core Scientific, TeraWulf, Cipher, Nebius.** Their equity is a levered claim on (a) a few tenants (often each other or OpenAI-linked), (b) capital markets staying open. Capital markets are closing right now: CoreWeave's 2031 notes reportedly yield 15-16% and its CDS is above 1,000bp (secondary sources, 2026-10-14/16).
4. **Oracle is a real business that has become a financing bet**: S&P cut it to BBB- (2026-07-09, secondary), FY26 FCF about -$23.7B (secondary), roughly half of $664B RPO tied to one customer (OpenAI, per S&P via secondary), and $260B of not-yet-commenced leases (S&P, secondary). 15.8x FY27 non-GAAP EPS looks cheap only if you ignore that.
5. **The best-structured new-build name is Hut 8**: investment-grade tenants, 15-year take-or-pay triple-net leases, $7.5B of non-recourse amortizing project debt (rated Baa2 on Beacon Point Phase 1). But the cash flows start in 2027-28 and bitcoin marks the P&L.
6. Fit with the investor's existing AI/semis load: the landlords add little extra AI beta (corr to the 6-name AI basket 0.24-0.36, QQQ beta 0.4-0.6); the AI-cloud names add a lot (beta 2.1-2.9, drawdowns -65% to -96%).
7. **Net call: one START-SMALL (EQIX). Everything else WATCH-WITH-TRIGGER or AVOID.** Lab evidence applies: momentum rank is the only selector with edge, and the AI-cloud names now have the worst 12-1 momentum in the group (CRWV -43%, ORCL -50%, IREN -36%, APLD -30%); that is information that they are not leaders, not a bargain signal.

## 6. Detailed plan: EQIX (the only START-SMALL)
**Business.** Retail colocation and interconnection (cross-connects, 9,700 net adds in Q2), plus the xScale hyperscale JV (equity-method $735M, $1.62B maximum VIE exposure). FACT (Q2 26): revenue $2.625B (+16%), adj. EBITDA $1.396B (53% margin), AFFO $1.168B (+20%), AFFO/sh $11.78 (+19%), MRR +11%, bookings $424M annualised (+23%).
**Thesis (1-2y).** Guidance was raised in July (AFFO/sh $42.69-43.29 from $42.31-43.11); recurring capex only ~3% of revenue; interconnection pricing power; lower rate sensitivity than peers (3y OLS -7% per +100bp vs AMT -16%); dividend growth tracks AFFO/sh.
**Priced in.** 22.9x AFFO, 4.4% AFFO yield, ~48% payout. Reverse arithmetic: guidance-level growth earns > 10% a year even with a 3-turn de-rating; no growth cushion if the multiple falls 5 turns.
**Three strongest bear points.** (1) AFFO yield 4.4% is 70bp below the 10-year; any further rise in yields compresses the multiple (5y EQIX range 1.19-2.91% dividend yield). (2) Growth is capex-funded: H1 FCF -$560M, capex $5-6B vs ~$4.3B AFFO and dividends $2.04B, so net debt keeps rising; the xScale JV is off balance sheet. (3) Hyperscaler self-build or AI-workload shift could slow retail interconnection growth (no evidence yet; 9,700 net adds record).
**Entry plan (three tranches of the intended position).**
- T1 (1/3, ~0.5% of net worth): after the Q3 print (date not yet verified; pattern suggests ~Oct 29) if FY26 AFFO/sh guidance is held or raised and price <= $1,020 (~23.7x).
- T2 (1/3): at <= $900 (~20.9x, AFFO yield 4.8%, dividend yield ~2.3%) or after two consecutive quarters of MRR growth >= 10%, whichever first.
- T3 (1/3): only if net debt/EBITDA <= 4.0x, 10y yield not above 5.5%, and price <= $1,050 on a later quarter's confirmation.
**Accumulate rule.** Add only on AFFO/sh guide maintained or raised; never add when new unsecured issuance prices above 6.0% or net debt/EBITDA > 4.5x.
**Trim/sell.** Trim above 27x FY26 AFFO (~$1,160) or if the position exceeds 2.0% of net worth; sell on thesis break below.
**Kill triggers (numeric/observable).** (a) FY AFFO/sh guidance midpoint cut or organic growth guided < 8%; (b) adj. EBITDA margin guided below 51%; (c) net debt/EBITDA > 4.5x; (d) a close below ~$760 (-23% from $986, inside the investor's 20-25% limit) triggers mandatory review/exit; (e) two quarters of net interconnection adds falling y/y.
**Cap and loss arithmetic.** Cap 1.5% of net worth (5y max drawdown -39%, worst 12m -34%): worst-case loss ~0.6pp of net worth at -40%, ~0.5pp at the -34% window. Combined with DLR/IRM, a landlord sleeve cap of 3.0% of net worth => ~1.2-1.5pp at a -40/-48% drawdown. For context from lab sizing arithmetic: sleeve share = 22.5% / 40% = ~56% of any dedicated sleeve, so EQIX alone cannot be 100% of a sleeve if the 22.5% limit applies to money invested in the sleeve.
**Model-estimate odds (12 months, not forecasts).** P(total return >= +30%) ~7%; P(>= +10%) ~45%; P(ends 12m at or below -25%) ~5%; P(touches -25% at some point in the 12m) ~12-15% (history: 17% of 12m windows over the last 5 years, an AI/rate-shock-heavy period). Lab priors on 5x outcomes apply: ~0%.
