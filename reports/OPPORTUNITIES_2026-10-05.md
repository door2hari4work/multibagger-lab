## What this cycle shows about the engine
1. It did its job: it stopped eight stretched momentum names from looking like opportunities. The adversarial step found real errors in the researchers' work (non-reproducible scenario arithmetic, stale price series, a mislabelled intraday price, missed insider selling, stale guidance) that were fixed as new thesis versions.
2. No name qualifies as an attractive entry; none was given an entry status. The home screen therefore shows 0 high-priority ideas, by design.
3. The lab's earlier evidence applies: momentum picks what has already moved; timing states carry no return edge (they describe entry price risk); the quality gate had no demonstrated value; 5x outcomes are rare.
4. India's regime filter is off (Nifty 500 below its 200-day average), which defers entries on all three India names that otherwise looked "setup forming".
5. Your portfolio is already heavily weighted to the AI theme (about a quarter directly, more through funds). A new AI-chain name adds to existing exposure, not diversification. Portfolio fit is computed privately at display time and is never stored in the repo.

## Known weaknesses (not hidden)
- Transcripts were mostly unavailable (IR sites blocked or 503); management remarks are tagged INFERENCE. Consensus estimates come from aggregators.
- India exchange announcement pages are blocked; documents came from company sites and the NSE static archive.
- The decomposed score uses declared, unvalidated weights; a high score never makes a thesis high priority (the dashboard also requires a surviving adversarial review, non-low confidence and a workable entry state).
- 22 of the 30 theses are Level-1 snapshots: a screen result, not research.
